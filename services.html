WINDSHIELD REPAIR

Small chip, fast fix.

Most chips and short cracks under 6 inches can be repaired in under 30 minutes with a resin injection that restores structural strength and stops the damage from spreading. Repairs are often fully covered by insurance with no deductible.

  • Star breaks, bullseyes, and combination chips
  • Short cracks (under 6")
  • Usually $0 out of pocket with comprehensive coverage
WINDSHIELD REPLACEMENT

When repair isn't enough, we rebuild it right.

Long cracks, damage in the driver's line of sight, or damage near the edge of the glass usually call for a full replacement. We install OEM-quality glass matched to your exact make and model, using manufacturer-spec urethane for a proper structural seal.

  • OEM-quality glass, correctly matched to your VIN
  • Manufacturer-spec adhesive and safe drive-away times
  • Lifetime warranty against leaks and workmanship defects

ADAS / SAFETY SYSTEM RECALIBRATION

Modern windshields are part of your safety system.

Many newer vehicles mount a forward-facing camera on the windshield for lane-keep assist, automatic braking, and adaptive cruise control. Replacing the glass without recalibrating that camera can leave those systems misaligned. We calibrate to manufacturer spec before we consider the job finished.

Included with replacement

ADAS recalibration is built into every windshield replacement on an equipped vehicle — it's not an upsell, it's part of doing the job correctly.
